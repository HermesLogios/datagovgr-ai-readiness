# What the broad reading of point 2.1 would add to the high-value datasets, snapshot of 2026-10-24

The portal harvests 11,938 map layers from the GIS servers of 31 public bodies (52.3% of the 22,805 datasets); 7,562 of them only show a map and 7 carry a high-value marking. Point 2.1 of the Annex adds to the high-value datasets all the environmental information of Directive 2003/4/EC, which covers measures such as plans and legislation that affect land and landscape (Article 2(1)(c)); the narrow reading (DECISIONS.md #17) leaves this clause out and does not count scans of single legal acts. Every layer is placed in a group by rule, from its title and the name of its source; the rule is checked on a random sample.

Narrow reading: the 3,383 layers of the group both readings are high-value by their content, without a marking in most cases. Broad reading: the 6,805 layers of the group broad reading only would be added, most of them scans of single legal acts of town planning, so that 10,188 map layers would be high-value instead of 3,383.
