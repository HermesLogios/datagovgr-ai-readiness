# Datasets of the Annex of Regulation (EU) 2023/138 on the portal, snapshot of 2026-10-24

66 datasets named by the Annex (`criteria/hvd_annex_items.csv`), searched in the title, the description and the tags of the 22,805 datasets and 127 data services of the snapshot. A match is a candidate; whether a dataset of the Annex is on the portal is judged by the reviewer. Present: the whole country, by one dataset or by several together, at the detail that the Annex asks; partly: some areas only, aggregates only, or part of the data; missing: nothing on the portal. A dataset that is missing from the portal may be published elsewhere, for example on the site of its producer.
