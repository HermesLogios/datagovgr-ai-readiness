# Decision log

Every decision that affects the methodology or the results is recorded here, with its date and rationale, so that the course of the project is fully traceable. The newest decisions come first.

| # | Date | Decision | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 16 | 2026-10-27 | The repository (documentation, code comments, console messages and generated reports) is kept in English. The deliverables to the supervisor (memo, final study) are written in Greek. The README opens with a Greek summary. | So that readers who do not know Greek can follow exactly what was done and how. The datasets' own titles and organisation names remain in Greek, as published by the portal. |
| 15 | 2026-10-24 | Link status (`archiver.is_broken`) and the MQA score (`qa.mqa_score`) are taken from the portal's own plugins and used as indicative measures for the whole catalogue. On the sample of 36 datasets the links are verified by independent download. | The portal's checker may fail for reasons unrelated to the publisher (e.g. blocking of automated requests, WMS services). Rates per organisation are examined before any conclusion. |
| 14 | 2026-10-24 | A dataset counts as machine-readable if at least one of its resources has a structured-data or geospatial-service format (list `MACHINE_READABLE` in `02_catalog_profile.py`). PDF, Word documents, images, HTML and archives do not count. | Corresponds to 3 stars on the 5-star scale and to the machine-readability requirement of Ministerial Decision 11654/2026. The list is public and open to revision. |
| 13 | 2026-10-24 | The automatic "download all" ZIP bundles that the portal generates for every dataset are excluded from the format, machine-readability and link counts. | They are not the publisher's choice and would double-count every dataset. They are recognised by the field `downloadall_datapackage_hash`. |
| 12 | 2026-10-19 | The usage of a dataset is measured by the page views in the portal's `tracking_summary` field (total and recent), and downloads by the sum of the corresponding counts of its resources. Ranking is done locally, after the snapshot. | The portal provides views per dataset and per resource but does not accept sorting by views through the API (HTTP 400, checked on 19/10). The counts cover visits to the portal, not API calls. |
| 11 | 2026-10-19 | Snapshots are stored as compressed JSON Lines files, with the records exactly as the API returns them, together with a manifest file and a SHA-256 checksum. | Verifiability and reproducibility. The size (about 30 MB per snapshot) allows storage in the repository. |
| 10 | 2026-10-19 | The snapshot includes every record type of the portal: datasets (`dataset`), data services (`data-service`) and data availability decisions (`decision`). The quality assessment covers datasets; data services are used in the API check of high-value datasets and decisions in the gap analysis. | The portal's search returns all three types. The counts per type are recorded in the snapshot manifest. |
| 9 | 2026-10-19 | Besides the study, an open assessment tool (web app) is developed with the same criteria. The checks are implemented once, in the `aiready` library, which both the scripts and the tool use. | The method becomes repeatable by others. The choice of tools is free, with transparency as the condition (guidance from the supervisor). |
| 8 | 2026-10-19 | Within each high-value category the datasets with the highest usage (views, downloads) are selected. If the portal provides no usage statistics, the selection is random and is recorded here. | Clarification by the supervisor of the criterion "the ones that have been requested". |
| 7 | 2026-10-18 | Licences: code EUPL-1.2; data and results CC BY 4.0. | The instructions require CC BY 4.0 for the data. The EUPL is the EU's open-source software licence. |
| 6 | 2026-10-18 | Two catalogue snapshots are taken: end of October and mid-December. | To record the changes during the deadlines of circular 24849 ΕΞ/03-07-2026. |
| 5 | 2026-10-18 | Every dataset is classified into one of three groups: "Ready", "Almost ready", "Not yet". The core criteria of each group are defined before the results are analysed. | Captures the "with little effort" of the meeting of 13/10, without adapting the rule to the findings. |
| 4 | 2026-10-18 | For every criterion that is not met, the effort to fix it is recorded: low (metadata and documentation only), medium (change in the file), high (change in a system or source). | Distinguishes the low-cost fixes, as requested at the meeting of 13/10. |
| 3 | 2026-10-18 | A grid of 23 criteria on three levels (quality 6, metadata and legal status 10, AI-readiness 7), scored 0, 1 or 2. | Follows the three levels of the supervisor's instructions. |
| 2 | 2026-10-18 | Sample of 36 datasets: 24 high-value (5 geospatial, 5 statistics, 5 mobility, 3 earth observation and environment, 3 meteorological, 3 companies) and 12 random from the other themes, with a fixed seed. | The instructions ask for 30-40 datasets; the meeting emphasised geospatial, statistics and mobility. |
| 1 | 2026-10-18 | Automated metadata checks are applied to the whole catalogue; manual checks only to the sample. | Full coverage gives a picture of the whole portal at low cost. |
