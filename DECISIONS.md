# Decision log

Every decision that affects the methodology or the results is recorded here, with its date and rationale, so that the course of the project is fully traceable. The newest decisions come first.

| 22 | 2026-10-29 | The numeric thresholds of the criteria are choices of the project: the shares of empty cells in Q2 (5% and 20%), the share of implausible values and the size of the manual review in Q4 (1%, 10 records), the tolerance of timeliness in Q5 (one period; one to three periods; 12 months), the minimum length of the description in M1 (100 characters), the number of recommended properties in M2 (six or seven; three to five), and the correspondence of the 5-star scale to the scores in Q6. Together with the core criteria and the rule of the groups (#20), they are applied to three datasets in the test of the criteria and fixed before the assessment of the full sample. | The sources define what is measured, not the level that is sufficient: ISO/IEC 25012 defines completeness, not an acceptable share of missing values. Stating the thresholds openly allows them to be examined and, if the supervisor asks, changed before they are fixed. Every change is recorded here. |
| 21 | 2026-10-29 | Assessment criteria, version 1.1. (a) New column `instruction`: the point of the supervisor's written instructions of 18/10 that each criterion implements; 15 criteria implement such points and 8 are additions, each with its source. (b) New columns `mqa_datagovgr` and `mqa_dataeuropa`: the indicators of the Metadata Quality Assessment (MQA) that check the same aspect, in the previous methodology, applied by data.gov.gr (405 points; indicators for 9 criteria), and in the current methodology of data.europa.eu (DCAT-AP 3, scores from 0 to 7.5; indicators for 14 criteria). (c) Additional sources: data.europa.eu data quality guidelines (Q2, Q3, AI1), Circular 24849/2026, section 1.5 (Q6), Regulation (EU) 2023/138, Art. 3(1) to 3(4) and Annex (M10, AI2). (d) M2: a property counts as present whenever it holds a value, including a broad, default or erroneous one, such as the whole of Greece for local data, a temporal coverage from 1900 to 2099 or a bounding box of the whole world or outside Greece. (e) M10: the exceptions of the Annex of Regulation 2023/138 apply: model data of numerical weather prediction through an API only (point 3.2(a)); historical versions of Earth observation and environment datasets through an API or a bulk download (point 2.2(a)). | (a) Makes the correspondence of the criteria with the instructions verifiable. (b) Circular 24849/2026 states that the national portal monitors the quality of metadata with the MQA and the 5-star scale. data.europa.eu replaced its methodology in 2026, while data.gov.gr still applies the previous one; both are therefore documented. (c) Each source was checked in its original text. (d) M2 checks the presence of properties of DCAT-AP, as the MQA does. The value from 1900 to 2099 appears in 19,458 of the 22,805 datasets of the snapshot of 24/10. (e) M10 checks the requirements of the Regulation, which include these exceptions. |
| 20 | 2026-10-27 | Assessment criteria, version 1 (`criteria/criteria.csv`; rules in `criteria/README.md`): 23 criteria, each with a written rule for every score, the method of checking and its sources. Almost ready: every core criterion below 2 can be fixed with low effort. Not yet: all other cases. Scores are reported per level as percentages, without an overall score. | Implements #3, #4 and #5. The core criteria and the rule of the groups are fixed before any dataset is scored, so that they are not adapted to the findings. The wording of the rules may be clarified after a test on three datasets of the sample; every such change is recorded here. |

| 18 | 2026-10-27 | Sample version 1, drawn with `03_sample.py` from the snapshot of 24/10 (`data/processed/sample.csv`; reserves in `sample_reserves.csv`). High-value group: in each category the eligible datasets are ranked by page views (ties: downloads, recent views, name) and the most viewed dataset of each organisation is selected until the quota is filled; the categories are filled in the order of #2 and a dataset is selected only once. Comparison group: random draw with seed 20261024 from the datasets without any high-value flag; a drawn dataset counted as high-value by content (#17) is skipped. Eligible: datasets with at least one resource of their own. | Implements #2 and #8 and the selection rules of the memo of 25/10. The limit of one dataset per organisation in each category keeps the sample of a category from repeating the practice of a single publisher, e.g. three weather stations of the same service, each published as a separate dataset. Reserves replace, in order, datasets removed from the portal before their assessment. The sample may be revised after the supervisor's comments on the memo; every revision is recorded here. |
| 17 | 2026-10-27 | A dataset without a high-value category in its metadata counts in a category when its content corresponds to a dataset of the Annex of Regulation 2023/138. The decision is taken by hand and recorded, with the Annex item and the reason, in `criteria/hvd_by_content.csv`. A dataset is examined when its title suggests Annex content (terms in `03_sample.py`) and it could enter the sample: enough page views for a category, or drawn for the comparison group. Two limits apply to the sample: the clause of section 2.1 of the Annex that adds all environmental information of Directive 2003/4/EC is not applied, and scans of single legal acts (e.g. the diagram of one town-plan amendment) are not counted as Annex datasets. | Applies the rule of the memo: high-value if marked in the metadata or if the content corresponds to the Annex. Examining all 22,805 datasets by hand is not feasible; the screening limits the work to the datasets that can change the sample. A dataset declared in one category may count in another by content, e.g. the Natura 2000 sites, declared as geospatial, are "protected sites" of earth observation and environment. Without the two limits, a large part of the about 12,000 map layers that the portal harvests from GIS servers of municipalities, regions and other bodies could count as high-value, and the comparison group would no longer represent the rest of the catalogue. The effect of the broad reading on the high-value scope of the portal is examined in the gap analysis. |

| 14 | 2026-10-24 | A dataset counts as machine-readable if at least one of its resources has a structured-data or geospatial-service format (list `MACHINE_READABLE` in `02_catalog_profile.py`). PDF, Word documents, images, HTML and archives do not count. | Corresponds to 3 stars on the 5-star scale and to the machine-readability requirement of Ministerial Decision 11654/2026. The list is public and open to revision. |
| 13 | 2026-10-24 | The automatic "download all" ZIP bundles that the portal generates for every dataset are excluded from the format, machine-readability and link counts. | They are not the publisher's choice and would double-count every dataset. | 12 | 2026-10-19 | The usage of a dataset is measured by the page views in the portal's `tracking_summary` field (total and recent), and downloads by the sum of the corresponding counts of its resources. The counts cover visits to the portal, not API calls. |
